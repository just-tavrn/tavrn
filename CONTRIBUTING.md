# Contributing

Contributions to tavrn follow the organization-wide
[contributing guide](https://github.com/just-tavrn/.github/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md) and the
[code of conduct](https://github.com/just-tavrn/.github/blob/main/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

For this repository in particular:

- It holds **documentation only** (overview, architecture, roadmap, self-hosting orientation, FAQ). Fixes,
  clarifications and better diagrams are welcome as pull requests. Keep the texts in English, link the
  [protocol](https://github.com/just-tavrn/tavrn-protocol) for anything normative instead of restating it, and
  keep the pages at the level of concepts.
- **Bugs and feature requests belong in the repository that owns the code** (the issue chooser links each of
  them). Questions and ideas go to [Discussions](https://github.com/just-tavrn/tavrn/discussions).
- **Security problems** are never reported in public: see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
- Contributions are accepted under the [contributor license agreement](CLA.md); the CLA bot asks you to sign it
  once on your first pull request.
